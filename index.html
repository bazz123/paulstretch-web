PaulStretch Web

Turn any sound into a slowly evolving ambient texture, right in your browser. A from-scratch reimplementation of PaulXStretch: nothing to install, and your audio never leaves your computer.

PaulStretch Web

The core time-stretcher.

  • Stretch from ×0.1 to ×10,000
  • 8 spectral modules: harmonics, pitch shift, spread, filter and more
  • Presets, built-in help, WAV export up to 10 minutes
Open the app

PaulStretch Web FX

Everything in the core app, plus effects for ambient and drone work.

  • Reverb with shimmer, 3-voice chorus, 3-band EQ
  • Drawable spectral EQ curve
  • Binaural beats with glide (headphones)
  • Streamed WAV export up to 3 hours
Open the FX version
